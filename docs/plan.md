## Stack and layout

TypeScript, Vite, Three.js (no React Three Fiber), vitest, ESLint. Node 24.

```
packages/core/        recipe and validator, poses and move library, deterministic engine, beat analysis,
                      edit commands, bundle builders, export range and target model constraints
packages/studio/      Vite app: viewport, characters, views, beat strip, export (MP4, WAV, zip)
schema/               pinned copy of the guide-bundle JSON Schema (owner: model-arena), now 0.4.0

docs/                 PRD, plan, friction.md, costs.md
```

## The guide bundle (contract with model-arena)

`model-arena/packages/core` owns the JSON Schema. This repo validates against a pinned copy, `schema/guide-bundle.schema.json` (version 0.1.0 written here from this table on 10 November 2026; 0.2.0 on 11 November adds the optional camera track). **To do:** re-sync `model-arena`'s schema with 0.2.0.

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `video` | MP4, H.264, 24 fps, frame count 8k+1; 1280×720 by default |
| `audio` | the track, trimmed to the exported range |
| `firstFrame` | PNG of frame 0 of the export; optional `firstFrameStyled` |
| `beats` | beat and downbeat times and BPM, re-based so times count from the export's frame 0 |
| `recipe` | the recipe that produced it (whole recipe, not trimmed) |
| `meta` | width, height, fps, frames, view, character, generator, licence per asset, `audioInVideo` |
| `camera` | optional (0.2.0): `camera.json`, per-frame position, target and vertical fov of the camera rig, with a suggested LTX `cameraMovement` (approximate) |

The bundle also carries `bundle.json`, a manifest naming each file. **Schema gap:** the schema has no field for the exported section of the recipe, so a mid-track range is written into the video asset's `name` ("Rendered guide, recipe frames 107-211 (4.458-8.833 s)"). Proposal for the next schema version: `meta.range { startFrame, startSeconds }`.

**Target model constraints** (700–4553 px per side, 24–60 fps, 3–30 s) sit in one small table in `packages/core/src/export-target.ts`. The sibling repo `model-arena` holds the full per-model spec database; re-sync the table with it.

Branch `feat/ms-core`. Three levels of evidence:

- placing library moves on beats from the palette; the five views render (screenshots);
- export at the 1280×720 default; a mid-track range (beats 8–16 of 20): 105 frames, H.264 1280×720 at 24 fps, AAC muxed, `audio.wav` trimmed so its first click lands on the first beat of the re-based `beats.json`;
- the inline size warning (832×480) and clip-length warning (under 3 s), neither blocking the export;
- portrait framing (720×1280) keeps outstretched arms in frame (screenshot, checked by eye);
- loading a generated skinned GLB with three embedded clips; Mixamo bone names recognised; clips sequenced on beats and exported;
- the inline confirm on switching to the loaded character: *Keep* leaves the library moves, *Clear* removes them; no browser dialog appears.

**Unit-test-only** (`npm test`, 46 tests):
- recipe validation and round trip; engine determinism and phase timing on the beat grid; pose speed classes;
- the edit commands `insertMove`, `swapMove`, `removeMove`, `setAmplitude`, `extendPhase`, `applyMotionCommand` (the UI calls them, but the smoke run drives only `insertMove`);
- beat analysis on synthetic click tracks; bundle JSON against the pinned schema; WAV encoding;
- export range resolution and 8k+1 snapping; target constraint warnings; move compatibility on a character switch.

**Built, not verified by any automated run:** FBX loading; drag and drop; audio playback; editing a selected move (amplitude, fps, blend, expression, swap, remove); applying recipe JSON by hand; the block figure; the dark background; exporting at 1920×1080 or 720×1280; custom sizes above 1920×1080 (H.264 level 5.1 is tried, untested).

**MS-9a, audio-reactive camera and motion (11 November, verified by `npm test` 68 tests, `npm run smoke`, `npm run examples`):**
- Camera rig (`camera.ts`, `camera-rig.ts`): modes static, orbit, push in, pull out, rise and tilt, lead (front of the subject), chase (behind), in a timeline per beat range. Orbit, dolly and crane speed follow the smoothed energy; every beat gets a push and a yaw nudge, downbeats a larger one, peaking on the beat. Rates are slew-limited, followers are critically damped with feed-forward, accent amplitudes are cut until their peak acceleration fits the clamp. Unit-tested: determinism, envelope, accents within one frame of the beat, acceleration clamps, chase framing.
- Audio drive (`audio-drive.ts`): per-frame energy (95th-percentile normalised, 0.12 s attack, 0.5 s release) and onsets (adaptive threshold, within one hop, about 12 ms).
- Reactive motion layer (`reactive.ts`, `accents.ts`, `perform.ts`) on top of the time-stretch path: per-bar tempo levels from energy (idle, half, normal, double, cross-faded at the bar), amplitude by energy, accent moves (hit, point, strike, kick, jump, spin) on onsets and downbeats with the key pose landing on the hit (a jump lands on it), per-joint lag for overlapping action and follow-through, seeded variation. Editable hit markers (`reactive.markers`) replace detection when present. Unit-tested: accents within one frame of onsets and downbeats, jump landing, same seed same output, different seeds differ, repeats differ, quiet bars idle, arms trail the hips.
- Studio: a Camera rig view; rig and reactive-moves controls; the top and orbit views draw the camera path, frustums at downbeat accents, a live frustum and the dotted floor path (hidden in exports). The fixed views stay switchable whatever the rig does. Browser-verified by screenshot (`npm run peek`, `npm run examples`).
- Export: `camera.json` (per-frame position, target, vertical fov, energy; the mode timeline; accents; a suggested LTX `settings.cameraMovement`, marked approximate). Values are the LTX list in `model-arena` (`providers/runware-api/models.ts`): dolly in, out, left, right, jib up, down, static, focus shift. Verified by `npm run examples`: A, C, D export with a camera track whose frame count matches ffprobe.
- Examples: `examples/recipes/{A,C,D}.json`; `npm run examples` regenerates the 48-beat track and the bundles in `examples/bundles/` (gitignored). D is the target form: the dancer and the camera follow the same beats.
- **Schema:** the pinned copy is now 0.2.0 (optional manifest `camera`, `$defs/camera`, asset role `camera`). **`model-arena`'s schema must be re-synced** before it reads these bundles; a 0.1.0 reader rejects the extra manifest key.

**Gaps:**
- Library moves do not drive a loaded rig: recipe poses need rest-pose retargeting onto the file's skeleton. A loaded character plays only its own embedded clips; the switch confirm offers to clear the library moves.
- No IK and no foot locking: feet can slide or sink slightly during blends; the bounce pose is solved by hand so the feet stay put.
- The `expression` field is validated, stored and exported, not rendered.
- MS-3 is a simple beat strip; there is no phase-block timeline, speed curve or before/after comparison yet.
- No CLI or MCP yet; the commands are a library.
- The range is not previewed: playback runs over the whole recipe.

### MS-9c, rigged mannequin and the audio clock (11 November)

Built (verified by `npm test` 86 tests, `npm run smoke`, `npm run examples`, `npm run peek` screenshots):
- The bundled rigged mannequin (Ecctrl AnimationLibrary, MIT; animations CC0, see `assets/characters/NOTICE.md`) is the default character; the procedural mannequin is the fallback.
- Clip performance (`clip-library.ts`, `clip-perform.ts`): loops time-warped so steps land on beats; accent clips as phases placed on the audio clock, the key frame (landing, impact) on the hit; amplitude per step or from energy; a hand-edited energy curve; retimed phases stored per marker.
- Energy drive: `reactive.drive` (default 1) scales how strongly energy sets playback (tempo level per bar, snappier steps when loud) and amplitude (bigger, heavier when loud, softer towards idle when quiet). Tested on the real clips: Dance_Loop at energy 0.9 against 0.2 shows about 8 times the pose speed, 1.6 times the knee range and 2.4 times the arm range.
- Timeline lanes (phases, moves, amplitude per step, a 230 px energy graph with pencil, point handles, snap to steps and a value readout; markers draggable), transport rate and preview fps, a drawn camera path, bones, x-ray, onion skin, arcs, the move gizmo, and a Mixamo-to-DEF retarget module (bone map and synthetic test).

Built from the owner's feedback (11 November, branch `feat/ms-core`; `npm test` 119 tests, `npm run smoke`, `npm run examples`, `npm run mixamo-check`):
1. **Smooth transitions** (`packages/core/src/transitions.ts`): eased crossfades between moves, never under 6 frames; a gait following a gait starts on the matching foot; accents fade in and out over at least 6 frames, a clip switch inside an accent crossfades over 8 frames, a later accent covers an earlier one. The blend per transition is a handle on the move lane (frames, 6–24) and a field in the inspector. Tested on the real mannequin: no joint pop above 30 degrees per frame across move changes, gait changes and back-to-back fight accents (the old code popped 88 degrees).
2. **Delete and undo**: Delete (close the gap) and Shift-Delete (hold), and a right-click menu, for moves, accent phase blocks, markers and steps (`packages/core/src/delete-edits.ts`; markers gain `skip` and `hold`). Undo and redo (Cmd-Z, Shift-Cmd-Z) for every recipe edit; one pointer press is one undo step. `app.ts` was split first (`app-view.ts`, `export/button.ts`).
3. **Sequences**: Save, Save as, Load, Delete, download and upload of the recipe .json, a browser library plus the committed `examples/recipes/` (new: `fight-combo`, `gait-change`). Every committed recipe reloads to identical frames (test). Fields documented in [recipe.md](recipe.md).
4. **Mixamo**: animation-only FBX/GLB clips are retargeted onto the rigged mannequin (drop, file picker, or `assets-local/mixamo/` in dev) and play as `mixamo/<name>`. `npm run mixamo-check` writes sheets, MP4s and metrics to `out/mixamo-check/`; verdicts in [friction.md](friction.md#mixamo-check-11-november-2026). Next: foot IK and travel speed per clip.

Then, also 11 November:
5. **Accent feel per marker**: "hit harder" (`impact`, 0–1: overshoot past the key pose, a deeper squash with the planted foot kept down, a hold of up to 3 frames on the impact frame) and the wind-up length (beats snapped to the step division, or frames; stored in `phases`), in a marker inspector and by Shift-dragging an accent's start edge in the phase lane.
6. **Per-bone sync for dance** (`reactive.sync`): additive layers on the mannequin, each with an amount: head nods on beats, shoulders and arms on onsets (the backbeat without a track), hips on the kick and bass (a new low-band envelope), with two-bone leg IK so the feet stay planted. Controls in the Reactive moves panel.

Known: `Sprint_Loop` at 120 BPM plays 1.33 times its authored speed and the loud-step snap adds a velocity kink at each step, so it reads jittery on its own (58 degrees per frame at its worst); a smoother snap curve is a small follow-up.

### Owner's six asks after trying the studio (11 November, branch `feat/ms-core`)

Verified by `npm run check` (typecheck of core, studio, CLI and MCP; lint; 166 tests) and `npm run smoke` (real headless Chrome, synthetic fixtures, ffprobe). Every UI edit goes through `applyMotionCommand` (`commands.ts`, table in `command-table.ts`); the studio's *Command log* shows them for replay.

1. **Audio lane and drum bands. Done.** `audio-bands.ts`: STFT spectral flux per band (low 30–150 Hz kicks, mid 1.5–6 kHz snares, high above 6 kHz hats) with an attack check, after the owner's camera-director DSP (rewritten here, not imported); mid onsets classed snare or hat by brightness; weak cross-band bleed dropped; amplitude peaks from the RMS. The tempo estimate now checks the double tempo against the kicks and snares (a kick/snare pattern at 120 BPM had read as 60). Audio lane: waveform (min/max per pixel), beat and downbeat ticks with bar numbers, onset markers per band, peaks. `beats.json` gains optional `onsets` and `peaks` (schema 0.3.0). Accuracy on synthetic patterns (±30 ms): kicks, snares and hats 100% precision and recall, mean timing error 1.7–2.8 ms (6.7 ms for quiet hats over a noise floor at 97 BPM). Not measured on real music.
2. **Clip editing. Done.** Moves are blocks (`atBeat` may be fractional; optional `lengthBeats`, `speed`): drag to move, drag either edge (longer plays slower; a drag past a neighbour rolls the cut), snap to beats, half beats, kicks, snares or hats (Alt: free); inspector fields start, length, speed, amplitude; *On nearest kick/snare*; *Place on every N kicks/snares* (an accent such as jump becomes hit markers). Commands `moveMoveTo`, `resizeMove`, `setMoveFields`, `snapMoveTo`, `placeOnEvery`, `set`. Undo/redo as before (one press, one step).
3. **Amplitude. Done.** Finding: the selected move's amplitude slider wrote the phase amplitude, which only the procedural figures read; the default rigged mannequin took its amplitude from the step lane or the energy only, so the slider did nothing there, and it stopped at 2 in steps of 0.05. Now the move amplitude (0–3) and the recipe `energy` multiply the rigged amplitude, with a fine slider and a number field. New `envelope` lane (130 px, 0–3): pencil, points (click to add, Shift-click to remove), start from loudness, kicks, snares, hats or peaks; stored as breakpoints; scales `frameAt` on every character (tested).
4. **Camera and paths. Done, with gaps below.** Follow rigs `lead`, `threeQuarter`, `side`, `chase`, plus `top` and `fixed`, with distance and height; *Start [mode] on [snare] N* (`startCameraMove`); a walk path drawn in the top view (`setRootPath`: smoothed, steady speed, facing along it, optional walk or run block over the chosen beats); the drawn camera path goes through `setCameraPath`. Blocked-out grid (1 m, bold every 4 m) and *Grid in export*. The export renders through the rig camera.
5. **CLI and MCP. Done.** See [agents.md](agents.md). `render` drives the studio in headless Chrome; verified once by hand (rigged, rig view, 97 frames) and by the same path in the smoke run.
6. **Abstract figures. Done.** `stick` (lines and joint dots) and `blob` (capsules, no visible joints) on the procedural skeleton; switching between procedural figures keeps every move (smoke-checked).

Not done or known limits:
- The audio lane shares the recipe's beat axis, so audio before beat 0 or past the recipe end is not drawn (use *Fit to track*).
- Band detection is heuristic (brightness ratio, coincidence rules) and untested on real mixes: expect missed kicks under a loud bass line, hats lost when they sit on a snare, and claps counted as snares.
- ~~Camera keyframes have no lane on the timeline~~: done below (Camera track).
- The walk path's speed is steady (or stepped by a travel lock), not energy-driven. A rigged gait now plays at the path's speed; the procedural walk and run have no authored travel speed, so their feet still slide (no foot locking).
- `placeOnEvery` takes library moves, rigged loops and accents; an imported Mixamo clip cannot be placed by it yet.
- The envelope scales the loops of the rigged mannequin, not its accent clips.
- The stick and capsule figures play recipe poses; the rigged mannequin's clips do not drive them.
- MCP `render` is not covered by a test (the CLI path it calls is).
- Schema 0.3.0 must be re-synced in `model-arena` before it reads bundles with onsets or the new figure kinds (now 0.4.0, below).

### Shot script, Action and Camera tracks, beat lock (11 November, branch `feat/ms-core`)

Direct a clip by speaking: an agent writes the direction as a shot script, the script lands on the timeline as an Action track and a Camera track, the owner fine-tunes by hand. Verified by `npm run check` (typecheck of core, studio, CLI and MCP; lint; 203 tests) and `npm run smoke` (real headless Chrome; the new step is `scripts/smoke-camera.mjs`); the worked example in [agents.md](agents.md#worked-example-directing-a-clip-by-speaking) was run by hand with `render` and its frames checked by eye.

1. **Shots.** `camera.segments[].shot`: wide, medium, close, low, top presets for distance, height, aim and fov, eased in over the block's first beat, `cut` to switch hard (`camera-shots.ts`). Command `setCameraSegment`. Unit-tested (eased, cut, the preset reached, unchanged without shots).
2. **Shot script.** `printScript` and `applyScript` (`shot-script.ts`, `script-apply.ts`): a view of the recipe, no second source of truth; grammar in [recipe.md](recipe.md#shot-script). CLI `describe --script` and `script`, MCP `get_script` and `apply_script`, command `applyScript`, and a Shot script panel in the studio. Round trip script → recipe → script is stable (test). Unknown words fail with the valid ones. **There is no fall move** in the library or the clips: `fall` is refused; the worked example rests under the top shot. Browser-verified: the panel applies a script and reads it back (smoke).
3. **Action and Camera tracks.** The timeline groups the phase, move, envelope, step and energy lanes under ACTION and the camera blocks and their automation lanes under CAMERA. Camera blocks are labelled with the move, amount, shot and lock, dragged and resized with the move blocks' drag code and undo (`moveCameraSegment`, `resizeCameraSegment`; a shared edge rolls the cut), snapped to beats, kicks or snares; a selected block opens the camera block inspector. Browser-verified: an edge drag rolls the cut, undo restores it, the inspector shows the block (smoke).
4. **Named camera moves with an amount (A).** `amount` (orbit degrees, dolly metres, truck metres, jib metres) or `from`/`to` (rise heights, push in or pull out to a distance) per block; the new mode `truck`. A block with an amount is deterministic and ignores the energy: orbit 360 over 4 beats ends exactly 360° on, at full intensity (test). Settable in the inspector, by command and in the script (`orbit 360`, `dolly in 2m`, `truck left 1.5m`, `rise 0.3m to 2m`). Unit-tested; the inspector browser-checked by screenshot only.
5. **Beat lock (E).** `lock` (beat, kick, snare, hat) and `strength` on move blocks, camera blocks and the travel along the walk path (`lock.ts`, `root-travel.ts`). Travel arrives at evenly spaced stations on each locked hit, eases in, holds, goes again; clips speed up into each hit and slow after it, keeping their length; camera blocks hold or punch in (`onHit`, `punch`) and an orbit with an amount still ends on its exact angle. `setRootPath` gains `gait: "glide"` (slide with no gait, on every figure) and a height `y` per point. Stations are red posts on the walk path and on the camera path in the top and orbit views. Unit-tested: determinism, stations within one frame of the onsets, strength 0 equals today's output exactly, path end unchanged, orbit exact under lock. Browser: the script's walk lock and the lock ticks on the camera lane are drawn (smoke screenshot `timeline-camera-track.png`); glide on the stick and blob figures is unit-tested only.
6. **Automation lanes (B).** `camera.lanes.distance|height|yaw|fov` on the envelope's breakpoint shape and its edit commands (each takes `lane`); one lane editor for all (pencil, points, snap, value readout, undo). A lane overrides its parameter where it has points (yaw adds an offset); `describe` reports them. Collapsible (Show: Envelope, Distance, Height, Yaw, FOV); a camera lane draws the planned value dashed. Browser-verified: the Distance lane drawn with the pencil (smoke).
7. **Tracks (C).** As in 3: the amplitude envelope sits under Action, the camera lanes under Camera.
8. **Record (D).** Arm a camera lane, play, move the Record slider: the played frames are written to the lane as one `drawEnvelope` command when playback stops. Browser-verified (smoke: a height lane recorded at 2.5 m). **Not built:** recording by dragging the camera in the viewport.
9. **Paths on the scene.** The walk path and the camera path were already drawn in the top and orbit views; now the parts belonging to the current camera block and action block are drawn brighter during playback, with the lock stations. Checked by eye in the smoke screenshots `paths-top-playing.png` and `paths-orbit-playing.png`.
10. **Schema 0.4.0.** The pinned schema's camera mode list (`$defs/camera` → `properties.modes.items.properties.modes.items.enum`) now matches `CAMERA_MODES`: adds `side`, `threeQuarter`, `top`, `fixed` (0.3.0 exports already wrote them and failed the repo's own schema) and `truck`; `BUNDLE_SCHEMA_VERSION` is 0.4.0. Nothing else changed: shots, amounts, locks and lanes stay out of `camera.json`, which already carries the camera per frame. A test validates every mode, and the smoke run validates the exported `camera.json`. **`model-arena` must take the same enum change.**

Follow-up, same day (`npm run check` 210 tests, `npm run smoke`, `npm run mcp-check`, and the README followed from a fresh clone):
- **Height cap.** The camera is held at 8 m (`CAMERA_MAX_HEIGHT`) whatever asks for more (a top block with a wide shot asked for about 11 m; a rise, a camera path or a height lane); `describe` names what asked. Unit-tested.
- **Paths in the script.** `on path (x,z) (x,z,height) …` and `camera: path (x,z,height) …` give the walk path and the camera path in metres, through `setRootPath` (`smooth: false`: the points as given) and `setCameraPath`; the script reads back with the points. CLI and MCP tested; `npm run mcp-check` runs the real MCP server over stdio through `apply_script` and `render` (headless Chrome, about 3–5 s for 193 frames), the first run of MCP `render`.
- **Public core.** `packages/core/src/index.ts` is the public entry (exports map `.`); every module stays under `./internal` for the studio; the core typechecks without the DOM library; `public-api.test.ts` imports only `@motion-studio/core` and computes frames and a camera in Node. README rewritten for a newcomer.

Then, for a self-contained demo repo: a script always prints the walk path's points (a drawn path is thinned to at most 24, within 15 cm at every beat when applied to an empty recipe; tested on a 200-point stroke), and **Load demo** generates the drum track in the browser and applies `examples/scripts/glide-path.txt` (smoke-tested). Nothing outside the repo is needed: the character is in `assets/characters/`, the track is generated, the scripts are in `examples/scripts/`; the only optional outside input is your own Mixamo clips in `assets-local/`.

Known limits: one walk path and one camera path per script; a script anchor names the first band an onset sits on (kick before snare).

### MS-9b, after D (next, in this order)

1. **Floor paths and beat-timed jumps.** One character per clip travels an editable floor path (a few control points, a smooth spline); travel speed follows energy; accent moves on the hits along the way (a jump that takes off and lands on the beat, a strike, a spin). Accent sets stay data, so a ninja combo or skateboard tricks are new entries.
2. **Waveform timeline.** Under the viewport: the waveform with the energy curve over it, and the hit markers (onsets and downbeats) drawn on it. Markers are editable (drag to move, click to add, delete, pick the accent per marker) and write `reactive.markers`; the playhead follows the viewport.
3. **Audio mapping controls.** Marker detection threshold, energy smoothing (attack and release), the energy-to-speed and energy-to-amplitude amounts, and a time offset to nudge everything a few milliseconds earlier or later.
4. **Blockout room.** An optional greybox scene (floor with a faint grid, a few walls, a ramp, pillars, flat neutral colours) for the floor-path and blocking examples; the lead and chase rigs work in it.
5. **Blocking mode and example B.** One to three figures on simple floor paths, translate and turn only, each in a distinct flat colour; example B pairs them with the reactive orbit and push-ins.
6. **Example E.** Audio-reactive running into ninja moves on the hits, in the room, with the lead or chase camera.

### Beat Path round 2 (13 November, branch `feat/beat-path`)

**The camera never loses the subject.** Cause: the rig aims at a follow point that is smoothed and acceleration-limited (8 m/s²), not at the subject; when the subject turned or sped up faster than that point could follow (a sharp corner, a long fast path in a 5 s clip, a locked dash), the aim fell metres behind and the subject left the frame, with or without audio; a drawn camera path that wandered off did the same. With a lock it was worse because stations were evenly spaced: a short gap between kicks got the same distance as a long one, a dash. Fixes: travel stations sit where steady travel would be on each hit (equal speed per leg); a framing guard (`camera.keepInFrame`, on in the Beat Path base) turns the aim towards the subject only near the frame's edge; lock hits within a quarter beat of a block's edge are already dropped. Tested on every frame: the examples, stress recipes (26 m path, few kicks, strength 1, each follow rig), no track with lock off for every camera word, a wandering camera path, every pair of block blends.

Also: muted hits, sound off, no-track playback, per-lane locks (chips) with next move on each hit, typed camera phrases (over shoulder, above, pace, then, at Ns), freeze, rows in seconds, shot description, figure colours, pen modes (no stray drawing), Delete and Escape, snap to beats or frames, the per-block camera track with beat ticks and hit rings on the map, a calm rig (no beat accents), a dark Runware theme with a paper map, Generate and Gallery placeholder sections, `exportGuideInMemory()`, the name constant. Not built: per-step travel options (glide, arc, hop, drop per step), parameter handles on the camera segments (radius, angles, distances), sub-frame editing, deliberate pass-bys.
